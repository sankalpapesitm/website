Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 9, 2026

Welcome to FraudShieldUPI ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and security. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and our website (the "Site").

[!IMPORTANT] Our Core Commitment: FraudShieldUPI is an on-device utility. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or monetize your personal messages, UPI addresses, contacts, or financial transaction logs.

1. Information We Access and Process

To protect your UPI payments from fraudulent activity, the App requires specific Android system permissions. All data read through these permissions is processed locally on your device in real-time.

  • Notification Listener Access: We scan incoming notification text (specifically from banking, SMS, and UPI payment apps) to identify fraud indicators such as unauthorized verification alerts, lottery prizes, or suspicious UPI deep links.
  • Camera Access (Optional): Required when you use the in-app QR code scanner to inspect the safety of payment destinations.
  • SMS Permissions (Optional): Used to scan existing message inbox history for pre-existing spam/fraud threats if requested by the user.

2. Data Storage and Transmissions

We believe in zero-data footprint principles:

  • On-Device Only: The analysis of VPA strings, message contents, and redirect loops takes place strictly on the client device.
  • No Cloud Uploads: No scanned messages, contacts, locations, or account IDs are ever uploaded to our servers or sent to third-party databases.
  • No Financial Log Collection: We never inspect or prompt for banking passwords, UPI PINs, security tokens, or credit/debit card numbers.

3. Analytics and Diagnostics

To optimize app performance and stability, we may log anonymous, non-personal diagnostic metrics (such as crash logs, API response latency, and app version numbers). These diagnostics do not contain transaction details or user-identifying markers.

4. Third-Party Links & Merchant Registries

When validating UPI addresses or QR merchants, the app queries official public API endpoints (such as NPCI registries) or crowd-sourced fraud databases locally. These endpoints only see the specific UPI handles queried and do not receive any user identification parameters.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When updates occur, we will post the revised policy on this page and update the "Last Updated" date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our security processes, please contact us via the Site's support desk or email:
